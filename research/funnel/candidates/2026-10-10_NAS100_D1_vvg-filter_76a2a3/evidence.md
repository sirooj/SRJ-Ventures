## Claim
Use the VVG premarket classifier to select unusual NAS100 days for intraday study.

## Raw evidence
As stated by the source (sample: IS/OOS walk-forward on 947 MNQ sessions 2021–2025, OOS folds with 30+ trades each): three simultaneously elevated premarket conditions (absolute overnight gap, absolute first-30-minute return, first-bar volume vs 20-day baseline) flag about 4.4% of sessions; flagged days reverse from the intraday peak before the close in 77.6% of cases with a mean peak-to-close giveback of 11.73 points.

## Counter-evidence
Eight directional configurations built on the classifier were tested and none passed (best T = 1.46, mean net +7.80 points over 127 OOS trades; 2024 broke year stability). The author keeps the classifier as a regime tag, not a signal. The sample is thin (40 flagged days, roughly 10 per year) and year-to-year behaviour differs sharply. Searched the abstract and full text for cost treatment: frictions are modelled, but the directional failure stands after costs.

## Mechanism
Large overnight gaps plus an energetic first 30 minutes plus heavy first-bar participation mark days when trapped inventory must be unwound through the session. The other side: intraday participants fading the emotional extreme.
