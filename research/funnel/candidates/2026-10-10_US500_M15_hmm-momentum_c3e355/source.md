Summary: A 2020 paper proposes Hidden Markov Models for intraday momentum on ES tick data from 2011. It adds volatility-ratio and seasonality side information through an input-output HMM. It reports the momentum effect intact after costs in simulation. The model uses no volume input and the test covers a single year.

> This paper has presented a viable framework for intraday trading of the momentum effect
