Summary: A study of NYSE stocks from 2001 to 2005 reports return continuation at half-hour lags that are exact multiples of one trading day. A daily decile spread earns a few basis points per half-hour with strong t-statistics. Volume shows similar periodic patterns. The test is cross-sectional, so a single-index port is untested.
